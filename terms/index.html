Membership

  • Coachwork Membership is an auto-renewing monthly subscription. It unlocks every finish, metal, numeral and hand style, dial engraving, all four instruments and the spoken greeting.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.
  • The membership renews automatically each month at the price shown in the app unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within the 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • You can manage or cancel your membership at any time in Settings > your name > Subscriptions, or from Coachwork's settings. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.
  • When a membership ends, your instruments return to the house finish. Your commission is kept, so it comes back if you rejoin.

Founders' Edition

The Founders' Edition is a one-time purchase that unlocks everything included in the membership for as long as Coachwork is available, with no renewal.

Using Coachwork safely

Coachwork's instruments are designed to be glanced at, not operated, while driving. Set up your instruments, rituals and automations while parked. Always obey traffic laws and keep your attention on the road.
