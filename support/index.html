We answer every message. Write to coachwork.team@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Add instruments to CarPlay

  • CarPlay widgets need iOS 26 or later.
  • On your iPhone, open Settings > General > CarPlay, choose your car, then Widgets.
  • Tap Add Widgets and choose a Coachwork instrument.
  • In the car, swipe to the dashboard to see it.

Add instruments to the Home Screen and StandBy

  • Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget.
  • Search for Coachwork and choose an instrument.

Set up the arrival chime

  • Open the Shortcuts app and tap Automation.
  • Tap New Automation, then choose CarPlay.
  • Choose Connects, then Run Immediately.
  • Add the action Play arrival chime from Coachwork.

Exterior says "Awaiting reading"

Exterior needs location access to fetch the weather. Open Coachwork, choose the Exterior instrument and tap Allow location. Readings refresh when you open the app and each time the arrival chime plays.

Itinerary shows "Clear"

Itinerary needs calendar access and shows timed engagements in the next 48 hours. All-day events are skipped.

Restore a purchase

Open Coachwork, tap the gear icon, then Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to the Apple Account you bought them with.

Cancel a membership

Open Coachwork, tap the gear icon, then Manage membership. Or open Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your iPhone.
