Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

Coachwork is made by Terra shpk ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the Coachwork app for iPhone does with your information. The short version: Coachwork has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. What you create in the app stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

Coachwork stores the following only on your device, in storage shared between the app and its widgets:

  • Your commission: the finish, metal, numerals, hands, dial signature and monogram you choose.
  • Your car's make and model, if you enter them for the nameplate.
  • The latest exterior conditions reading, so the widgets can show it.
  • Your preferences, such as whether the spoken greeting is on.

We never receive any of this. It is removed when you delete the app.

Location

If you allow it, Coachwork uses your approximate (city-level) location to show exterior conditions on the Exterior instrument. To do that, your approximate location is sent to Apple's WeatherKit service, which returns the weather. We do not receive, store or share your location. Apple's handling of WeatherKit requests is described in Apple's privacy policy at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

Location access is optional. Every other part of Coachwork works without it.

Calendar

If you allow it, the Itinerary instrument reads your calendars on your iPhone to show the time and title of your next engagement. Calendar data is read on the device and never leaves it.

Calendar access is optional.

Battery level

The Reserve instrument reads your iPhone's battery level on the device to draw its gauge. It is not stored or sent anywhere.

Purchases

Memberships and the Founders' Edition are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment; we never see your payment details. The app checks your purchases on your iPhone with Apple's StoreKit to unlock members' features.

Sounds and the spoken greeting

The arrival chime is played from a file inside the app. The spoken greeting is generated on your iPhone by Apple's speech synthesis. Nothing is recorded, and Coachwork does not use the microphone.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect analytics or usage data.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We don't track you across apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share personal information.
  • We don't include third-party SDKs.

Third parties

The only third party involved is Apple, through WeatherKit (if you allow location) and the App Store (if you make a purchase). Both are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Keeping or removing your data

Your data is kept on your iPhone for as long as the app is installed. To remove it, delete Coachwork from your iPhone. To withdraw consent for location or calendar access at any time, open Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services or Calendars and change Coachwork's access.

Because we don't hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. You are still welcome to contact us with any privacy request, and we will answer it.

Children

Coachwork is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
